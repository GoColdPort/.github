## Welcome to ColdPort
ColdPort is bridging the gap in America's $46B temperature-controlled logistics market. We develop, own, and operate state-of-the-art port-centric cold storage facilities at critical global gateways.

### 🏢 Our Mission
To eliminate the "middle mile" by constructing next-generation thermal envelopes directly at major seaports and airports, ensuring pharmaceutical and agricultural integrity while achieving Net Zero emissions by 2035.
